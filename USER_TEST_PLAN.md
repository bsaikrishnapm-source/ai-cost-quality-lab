# Usability test plan — not yet conducted

## Decision to inform

Can a product practitioner explain why an option qualifies, how review costs change its economics, and when no recommendation is justified?

## Participants and privacy

Recruit 3–5 consenting product or operations practitioners. Obtain permission before recording. Use anonymous participant IDs in any published findings. Do not collect employer-confidential inputs or publish names or quotes without consent.

## Tasks

1. Without coaching, identify the baseline recommendation and explain why the other options fail.
2. Double review cost and explain the cost and margin changes.
3. Save baseline and doubled-cost scenarios and compare them.
4. Set a 99% quality floor and explain the appropriate next decision.
5. Change an input and export an up-to-date result.

## Record per task

Independent completion, assistance needed, time, observed confusion and participant explanation. Ask what the participant believes is real versus hypothetical. Record findings separately from interpretations.

## Proposed decision criteria

Treat any misunderstanding of synthetic data as a high-priority communication defect. Investigate any task needing assistance from two or more participants. These small-sample thresholds guide iteration, not statistical claims.

## After sessions

Publish anonymized observations, limitations, the chosen change, rejected alternatives and a follow-up check. Current status: no participants recruited, no sessions completed, no findings asserted.
